\subsection{Normalized and Offset Min-Sum}
% Sources: 
%       Chen & Fossorier (2002). Near Optimum Belief Propagation Based Decoding, IEEE Comms.
To compensate for Min-Sum's overestimation:

Normalized Min-Sum:
$$
m_{j \to i} = \alpha \cdot \left( \prod \text{signs} \right) \cdot \min \left| m_{i' \to j} \right|
$$
Where $\alpha \in (0,1]$ is a normalization factor (e.g., 075).

Offset Min-Sum:
$$
m_{j \to i} = \left( \prod \text{signs} \right) \cdot \max \left( \min \left| m_{i' \to j} \right| - \beta, 0 \right)
$$
Where $\beta$ is a small offset constant to reduce overestimation.
